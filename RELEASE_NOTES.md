First release of Pixel Spot, a PixelPlayer-style remote for Spotify.

- Browse your playlists, liked songs, albums and search, and control playback through the Spotify app
- Colours from album art, your wallpaper, a fixed colour, or a theme: Rosé Pine, Gruvbox, Catppuccin, Nord, Atom One or Zinc
- Add the current song to your playlists, with a check for songs already in them
- Optional lock screen player with a clock and an always-on mode

You need the Spotify app, Spotify Premium and your own free Spotify developer app. The README walks you through it.
